Privacy policy

One Hundo, November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. One Hundo has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't track you or share anything with anyone.

Where your data lives

Your challenges and attempts are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices and come back after a reinstall. Only you can see them there; the developer can't. Apple keeps them under your Apple account's own privacy terms.

To stop syncing, turn off One Hundo in Settings, your name, iCloud. To remove the data from iCloud, delete it there under Manage Storage. Your iCloud or computer backup also includes the app's data, like other apps'.

Reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in the iPhone Settings app, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage numbers. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
